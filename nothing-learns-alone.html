Nothing Learns Alone

A lesson becomes knowledge only when it is stress-tested against another system. This is every such seam in one real corpus — 269 of them, drawn from 568 lessons across 124 projects. 197 rest on a single source. 28 projects have never been tested against anything at all, and never join the body.

Play: you are the system. Steer with the pointer and touch a project to absorb it. A seam closes when you hold both of its ends — so what you score is not what you collect, it is what you connect.

Edges come from Composed: citations. A known parser flaw can read an illustrative mention as a genuine composition, so a few seams are spurious; and because the graph records no negative edges, a pair shown as untested may in fact have been tested and failed. Sound on.
